# Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis

Revenue grew significantly in fiscal 2024 driven by strong demand in our cloud segment. Operating expenses also increased due to continued investment in R&D and go-to-market headcount.

We believe our diversified customer base and expanding product portfolio position us well for continued growth in fiscal 2025, notwithstanding macroeconomic headwinds affecting enterprise spending.

## Results of Operations

The following table summarizes our consolidated results for the periods presented.

| Line Item | FY2024 | FY2023 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue | 4,235,102 | 3,812,004 |
| Cost of revenue | 2,100,000 | 1,950,000 |
| Gross profit | 2,135,102 | 1,862,004 |
| Operating income | 812,300 | 690,150 |

Gross margin improved year over year, reflecting cost efficiencies in our data center operations and favorable product mix shift toward higher-margin subscription revenue.
